# User Personas and Use Cases

## Primary Persona: Solo Student Creator

The primary user is a student creator publishing educational, personal brand, or niche content across YouTube and Instagram. They are learning what works, experimenting with formats, and need a private place to track every published piece of content.

### Needs

- Save links quickly after publishing.
- Remember the topic, hook, CTA, and intent behind each post.
- Track views, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, and follower growth manually.
- Compare performance across content types.
- See which topics are worth repeating.
- Keep the system private and lightweight.

### Constraints

- Limited time.
- May not have API access or analytics exports.
- Needs simple forms and clear dashboards, not enterprise analytics.
- May use a phone for quick updates.

## Secondary Future Persona: Creator Using Multiple Platforms

The future user is a creator who publishes across more than YouTube and Instagram and wants a unified performance archive. This persona is not the MVP target, but the data model should avoid choices that block future platform expansion.

### Future Needs

- Additional platform support.
- Optional API integrations.
- Cross-platform comparison.
- Exportable reporting.
- More advanced tagging and grouping.

## Use Cases

## Add New Content

The creator publishes a video, Short, or Reel, then pastes the URL into CreatorVault. The system detects the likely platform and type. The creator edits metadata, adds topic and notes, and saves the item.

## Track Stats Manually

The creator returns after publishing to record metrics at recommended intervals such as Day 0, Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, and Day 30. The system stores each snapshot and updates charts.

## Review Performance

The creator opens a detail page or dashboard to see how content has performed over time. They review latest metrics, trend charts, engagement rate, and snapshot notes.

## Compare Content

The creator filters the library by platform, content type, topic, tag, date range, or performance. They compare similar content items to understand what worked.

## Identify Best-Performing Topics

The creator reviews topic-level summaries to see which topics drive the highest views, engagement, or growth. They use this insight to plan future content outside CreatorVault.

## Maintain Personal Creator Portfolio

The creator uses CreatorVault as a private archive of work. Each item contains the original URL, context, hook, CTA, notes, tags, and performance history so the record remains useful even after platform dashboards change.
